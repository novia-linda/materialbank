Reporting, screenshots and safe finishing

Open the Evidence Lab

The normal route is one level → one checked PDF → Moodle. No account or server stores your reports in this app.

During a level

  • Record the actual answer in a short summary. Preserve the important condition, date or exception. Do not paste a whole conversation.
  • Select only original sources you personally read and enter exact IDs or fields. For example: E03; C08 or ORD-1053, DeliveryNote.
  • Add your own two screenshots. Read the field-specific instructions; optional teacher examples are guidance, not your evidence.
  • Use Download backup before an interruption. It includes images and text. Keep your editable policy, FAQ and Sheet in your own account as well.

Before leaving

  1. Preview the A4 report. If text does not fit, shorten the named field without removing an essential fact. Do not shrink the whole page to unreadable text.
  2. Download the level PDF. Open the saved file, not just the preview. Check your name, five test rows, source references and both images.
  3. Upload this PDF in Moodle and retain previous level PDFs there. Before the deadline, return to add the next level. If the submission is unexpectedly locked, contact the teacher.

Connection and storage

After this app is fully loaded, typing, image input and PDF generation run on the device. If internet stops, keep this tab open and download your work. Opening Gemini, fetching new sources and submitting to Moodle still need internet. Reopening this website offline is not promised.

The local draft is a convenience, not permanent storage. Private browsing, browser-data clearing, device changes or storage errors can remove access to it. The save indicator reports whether the local write succeeded. A downloaded backup is the way to continue on another device; a PDF is not an editable backup.

Equivalent route when technology fails

Keep the actual results, including failures. Use a backup or the HTML/print export if possible. The independent templates below are also available without the app. Supply the same evidence; a second page is acceptable in this fallback when needed for readable screenshots. The route does not reduce your mark.

What the progress indicators mean

Completed fields are not an automated mark. The teacher still checks whether the answer, evidence and screenshots demonstrate the work. The app permits honest technical limitations and exports unfinished drafts; never turn a failed update test into a success just to finish.
