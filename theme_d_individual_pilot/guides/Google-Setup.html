LEVEL 1Create your Gem and add the policyFirst setup: your instructions and the first knowledge file.

Use during Level 1. Start with the policy and task instructions for that level. You do not need the email files, call transcripts or order register yet.

Create your first Gem

  1. Open Gemini in your browser. Use the sidebar: Gems → New Gem.
  2. Give it a name and enter your own instructions. Under Knowledge → Add files, add your working policy. Save the Gem.
  3. Use that saved Gem for the tests. When updating knowledge later, edit this Gem rather than creating a new one. Keep source citations enabled if the option is available.

Interface reference: Google: create, use and edit Gems. A file in one ordinary chat is not the same as a source saved in the Gem. This course deliberately makes you maintain the saved configuration.

Later levels use the same Gem. You add or update knowledge as the assignment progresses. You do not need to set up all four levels now.

LEVEL 2Turn emails and calls into an FAQA separate analysis chat, then your FAQ in the same Gem.

Use during Level 2. Continue with your Level 1 Gem. This is where the email and telephone material is introduced.

Keep analysis separate from testing

  1. Use a normal analysis chat in Gemini to help process the original email and phone files. Keep this chat separate from tests of your saved Gem.
  2. Create your own five-entry FAQ using the knowledge-entry template or the same structure in Google Docs. Read the original records to check the answers, conditions and E/C references. Include an owner, version and review date.
  3. Add your FAQ as a Knowledge file beside the policy in the Gem. Do not add the raw email or call logs to the working Gem in this exercise. Keep the originals for your own checking.
  4. Save the Gem and open a fresh conversation with it for a test. Use the five questions in your Level 2 instructions and reporting workspace.

When you correct an FAQ entry: update the Gem's knowledge source, not just one test conversation. If Drive does not refresh, replace the FAQ attachment manually and record that route.

The Level 2 assignment contains the full FAQ requirements, helper prompts and screenshot instructions. This section only explains which Google workspace to use.

LEVEL 3Add the register and test an updateYour working Sheets copy and the source-update experiment.

Use during Level 3. The order register starts here. You do not need to prepare a spreadsheet during Start here or Levels 1-2.

Make your own working register in Sheets

  1. Download 04_Order-Register.xlsx from the Level 3 instructions and upload it to your own Drive.
  2. Open the Excel file in Sheets. Use File → Save as Google Sheets to create a native Sheets version. Give this working copy a recognisable name.
  3. There are now two files. Continue in the native Sheets copy, not the unchanged Excel upload. Inspect the Read me, Orders and Data dictionary tabs.

Conversion creates a separate Sheets file; it does not change the original Excel upload. Reference: Google: Excel and Sheets.

Try adding the working Drive source

In the Gem Knowledge area, try Add from Drive and select the same working register you are editing. Google requires Keep Activity and a Google Workspace connection for this route. If prompted, review the connection before proceeding. Use only the fictional training files. Google reference.

If your account does not offer the register in the picker, or the file is not read usefully, do not buy a subscription. Use the manual-file route below and record the limitation. This exercise does not require the paid Gemini sidebar inside Drive or Sheets.

Then follow the Level 3 task: test the original register first. Add the specified new test order afterwards and observe what the Gem can find. Keep the original order rows unchanged.

LEVEL 4Update the knowledge and test againRevise the FAQ and policy, add a register field and retest.

Use during Level 4. Return to the Gem, FAQ, policy and working register you have already created. Do not begin again.

Update the existing knowledge sources

  1. FAQ: return to the original emails and calls for the less-common situations. Add or improve the relevant FAQ entries and retain their original record IDs.
  2. Policy: apply M01 to P10 in your editable policy, mark the working policy as version 2.0 and leave P01-P09 unchanged. Check whether the FAQ repeats the old rule. Replace the superseded active policy in the Gem with your updated version.
  3. Register: follow M02 to add PreferredUpdateChannel to your existing Orders sheet. Enter the five specified values and update the data dictionary. Leave other cells in the new column blank: blank means Not recorded. Keep existing statuses, dates and notes unchanged.
  4. Retest: use fresh conversations with the same saved Gem. Check the updated sources and record what the Gem actually uses. You may try the update again after a short interval; automatic updating does not have to succeed.

Keep the management update outside the tested Gem. It contains the new register values. Use it as original evidence for your edits, not as an additional Gem source. The updated policy supplies the rule; the edited register must supply the order values.

The Level 4 assignment gives the exact changes, order IDs, test questions and screenshots. It also explains why the Level 1-3 answers remain valid after the policy update.

LEVELS 1-4Accounts and the three workspacesWhat belongs in Gemini, your Drive and the Evidence Lab.

Reference for Levels 1-4. These are the workspaces used across the assignment, not three things to set up during Start here.

Use a personal Google account, Gemini in a desktop web browser, Drive and Sheets. The assignment does not require you to buy a plan. Menus and account limits can change; the fallback routes below protect your work without pretending that an unavailable feature succeeded.

LEVELS 3-4Run or repeat the update experimentCheck the source, test in a fresh Gem conversation and record the actual result.

Use in Level 3 or Level 4 when that level asks you to change and retest the register.

  1. Make the required change in your working Sheet and confirm that the file has saved. Keep a screenshot of the changed row or field.
  2. Ask the specified question in a new conversation with your saved Gem. Do not paste the new answer into the test chat.
  3. Record the actual reply. Continue with another task, then optionally try once more and record the approximate wait. A correct-looking answer alone is not proof that the updated file was read.
  4. Distinguish: found without re-upload; not found; found only after manual re-upload; or unclear. Correctly record failures as well as successes.

Google says a Gem uses the most recent version of a source added from Drive, but it does not specify a guaranteed one-minute refresh time. This is a source-update experiment, not a certified live-system connection. Google reference.

What matters: document the source change and the test honestly. An unsuccessful automatic update is still a result you can report.

IF NEEDEDManual-file fallback for the registerReplace the edited file yourself and record that this was a manual update.

Use only when needed in Levels 3-4. This is an alternative route after editing the register, not another compulsory task.

  1. In the edited working Sheet, use File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx). Add that new export to the Gem and remove the superseded register snapshot from its active knowledge.
  2. If XLSX cannot be read, export the current Orders tab as CSV. Alternatively, copy the relevant updated rows with their column headers into a clearly labelled text file.
  3. Retest and record manual re-upload. Do not describe a successful manual replacement as proof of automatic refresh.

Do not re-add the original course TXT/CSV after an edit and expect it to contain your changes. Those downloads are baseline snapshots. References: Google: supported uploads; Google: exporting a spreadsheet.

ALL LEVELSPrivacy, account limits and when to stopUse fictional data. Preserve your work if a service restriction prevents progress.

Reference for all assessed levels. Preserve your files and report any genuine service limitation.

Personal Gemini account data is governed by Google's consumer terms and privacy controls. Activity settings affect data use; do not treat the account as an approved environment for real customer information. All course records are invented. Do not include passwords, account menus or real customer details in screenshots. Google: Gemini privacy.

If you encounter a temporary quota or account restriction, save a backup and your source files. Retry later or use the course fallback and explain the obstacle. Do not report invented AI results. The teacher assesses the work and evidence, not your ability to remove a service restriction.
