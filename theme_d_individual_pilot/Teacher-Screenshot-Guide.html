Prepare, publish and update

  • Use the exact lowercase filename and a real PNG image. Do not just change a JPG extension to .png.
  • Crop irrelevant browser space. Redact private details and answers with solid opaque shapes, then export a flattened image.
  • Upload into examples/. Keep your existing images when updating the app package; the ZIP intentionally does not provide them.
  • After publication, reload the app. Replace a file with the same name to update it, or delete it to remove its link. Missing or unreadable files produce no broken-image link.
  • Examples never satisfy a student screenshot requirement or get copied automatically into a report/backup. The guide does not turn an example into a model answer.
