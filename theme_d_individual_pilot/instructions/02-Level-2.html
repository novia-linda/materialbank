Create five recurring FAQ entries

Include exact E and C record IDs. Distinguish customer requests from approved staff replies. Do not invent rules or treat a one-off example as a universal policy.

How to verify an FAQ entry

For each claim, open the cited original record and read the whole exchange. Check the action, any conditions, required information and what must not be promised. Combine wording variants; do not erase a condition that changes the answer. Keep uncommon information available in the raw records for later.

Check this draft FAQ against the attached originals. Which sentence is not fully supported? Has a condition, time limit or human approval been lost? Show the original record IDs. Suggest a correction, but do not add company facts from general knowledge. MY DRAFT: [paste your entry]

Add your FAQ to the Gem, then test

Open the five Level 2 tests

  1. Copy one question from this level of the reporting app and ask your saved Gem. Use a new conversation with the same Gem for each main test and for an important retest, so an earlier pasted answer does not supply missing knowledge.
  2. Read the response. Record Correct / Partly correct / Incorrect or no answer for the first attempt. Write a short summary of what it actually said. A refusal can be correct when the source says a person must decide.
  3. Open the original policy, email, call or register. Select every source you personally checked and enter an exact ID, section or field. A citation displayed by Gemini is not a substitute for reading that source.
  4. If the answer is wrong or incomplete, locate the missing information, improve the appropriate knowledge or instruction and retest. Keep the first result and record the latest result; do not rewrite the first attempt as a success.
  5. If a genuine tool limitation remains, record it honestly. Do not tick Correct merely to finish. A reporting app can check that fields are filled in; it cannot mark the content for the teacher.

When a test reveals a missing condition, fix the relevant FAQ entry and update the Gem's source. Do not teach the correct answer only inside one test conversation. If Drive does not refresh, replace the FAQ attachment manually and record that route.
