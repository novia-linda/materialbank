A general policy cannot tell you the status of an individual order. Add the supplied operational register and distinguish a recorded fact from an estimate, a blank field and a live system.

Test the five fixed-register questions

Open the Level 3 tests

  1. Copy one question from this level of the reporting app and ask your saved Gem. Use a new conversation with the same Gem for each main test and for an important retest, so an earlier pasted answer does not supply missing knowledge.
  2. Read the response. Record Correct / Partly correct / Incorrect or no answer for the first attempt. Write a short summary of what it actually said. A refusal can be correct when the source says a person must decide.
  3. Open the original policy, email, call or register. Select every source you personally checked and enter an exact ID, section or field. A citation displayed by Gemini is not a substitute for reading that source.
  4. If the answer is wrong or incomplete, locate the missing information, improve the appropriate knowledge or instruction and retest. Keep the first result and record the latest result; do not rewrite the first attempt as a success.
  5. If a genuine tool limitation remains, record it honestly. Do not tick Correct merely to finish. A reporting app can check that fields are filled in; it cannot mark the content for the teacher.

Verify in the Orders sheet using OrderID and field names. A row number alone is not enough because rows can move when sorted. The original 30 rows must remain unchanged so these answers stay checkable later.

Add one new test order and observe the update

This experiment is separate from the five fixed-register questions. Add one row with OrderID = ORD-TEST-01. Choose a fictional name, a mainland-Finland city and a documented status. Use a clear LastUpdate and note, such as Technical update test. Do not use real customer data.

  1. Save or wait for the Sheets save indicator. Take Screenshot 1 of the new row and its key fields.
  2. Ask the Gem in a fresh conversation: What status and delivery note are recorded for ORD-TEST-01? Do not paste the row or its expected answer into the question.
  3. Record whether the new information appears. While preparing your notes, allow a short interval and try once more; record any approximate wait. There is no guaranteed refresh time.
  4. Take Screenshot 2 of the actual response. If nothing new appears, record that. You may try replacing the attachment manually, but mark a manual replacement as manual, not automatic.

Important: A correctly edited source and an honest tested observation matter. Automatic updating does not have to succeed. If access prevents testing at all, record the exact limitation rather than inventing a result. Keep evidence of the edit.

Record the update attempt in the app's experiment block. Explain briefly what your test can and cannot prove. One successful response does not demonstrate a reliable real-time integration.
