Eyebrow: AI for Business | Theme D | Individual work - overview

Time budget: 2-3 minutes for the technical practice; allow roughly 2-3 hours for each chosen level.

Submission: One separate PDF per completed level. No submission for Start here.

In Theme C you worked with customer feedback. In Theme D you test a possible information-support solution in the same general customer-service setting. The company is fictional and has no required product sector. It handles orders and customer questions through Customer Information, Orders & Delivery, Billing & Payments and Technical Support. You do not need to have found a particular result in Theme C.

Build one internal knowledge assistant for a new customer-service colleague. The colleague uses it to find information while helping customers. It is not a customer-facing chatbot and must not make bookings, change orders or approve refunds.

Section: UNDERSTAND THE WORKFLOW

Section text: Google is where you build. The reporting app is where you document.

Know the three places you will use

  • Gemini, Drive and Sheets: build and keep your own assistant and working sources, using your personal free Google account. No paid subscription is required by the assignment.
  • The Evidence Lab on GitHub: record tests, check sources, add your own screenshots and generate a PDF. There is no automatic link to Gemini or to a cloud drive.
  • Moodle: read the instructions and submit the finished PDFs. This is where your teacher receives your work.

Use only the supplied fictional data. Work on your own copies. Do not put real customer records, passwords or personal account details into the assistant or screenshots.

Try the reporting tool before the real work

Open Start here in the Evidence Lab

  1. Type your name.
  2. Add two non-private images, or use the two built-in demo-image buttons. They do not need to show a Gem.
  3. Type a few words in each demo text box and try the checkbox. There are no knowledge questions and no correct or incorrect answers here.
  4. Download DEMO-Do-Not-Submit.pdf. Open it and check that your name, both images and your words are visible.

Important: The demo PDF is a technical test only. Do not upload it to Moodle or submit it anywhere. It is not assessed.

Output: You have tried image input, text input and PDF export. You can now concentrate on the actual assignment.

Prepare one level at a time

Plan to finish a level, download the PDF, open and check it, and upload it to Moodle in one study session. This is a recommendation, not a penalty for taking a break. Save a backup before stopping unexpectedly.

The app keeps a local draft when the browser allows it. It is not long-term storage and does not synchronise across devices. Your permanent copy is the file you download and keep. Do not assume that closing a browser, using private browsing or clearing site data will preserve your draft.

Keep your original source files and all downloaded PDFs in a folder you can find again. At higher levels, keep a separate current working policy, your FAQ and your order register. Do not use the reporting app to store these source documents.

Google access or upload difficulties

Read the Google setup and fallback guide. It distinguishes the free Gemini web app from paid Gemini features inside Drive/Docs. Check access before allocating a long work session. If a feature or quota blocks you, save your work, use a supported file alternative or document the limitation. You do not need to buy a plan to satisfy this assignment.
