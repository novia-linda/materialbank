Theme D Level 1 | Build your assistant

Eyebrow: AI for Business | Theme D | Level 1 | 3 points

Time budget: approximately 2-3 hours, including setup, tests and the report.

Submission: D1-Knowledge-Assistant.pdf

Create a Gem that helps a new staff member find approved company information. You decide its behaviour; the policy supplies the facts. You do not need a completed group Gem or a specific Theme C result.

BUILD THE FOUNDATION

One policy, one Gem and five checked answers.

Read the policy and identify the assistant's limits

Company policy - PDF | Editable policy - Word | Plain-text alternative

Read P01-P10. Note the difference between an order record, an estimate and a decision requiring a person. Keep the section IDs: you will use them to verify answers. Choose the PDF for the initial knowledge upload; keep the editable copy for later maintenance. Do not upload every format of the same policy.

Output: You can describe the assistant's purpose and at least two things it must not do.

Write and save your own Gem instructions

Open Gemini in your browser, using your personal Google account. Open the sidebar, choose Gems and New Gem. Name it something clear, such as My Customer Service Helper. Write your instructions using the structure below, then add the policy under Knowledge and save the Gem.

PURPOSE [Who uses this assistant, and what should it help them do?] SOURCES [Which approved sources may it use for company facts?] MISSING INFORMATION [What should it do rather than guess?] BOUNDARIES [Which actions and decisions belong to people?] ANSWER FORMAT [Describe a short useful reply, a source reference and any necessary next step.]

Keep company facts in the policy rather than copying the whole document into Instructions. The instructions should guide behaviour. Use short professional replies for an internal colleague, with the relevant source section and any important limitation.

Use AI to improve wording, not to invent rules
I am designing an internal customer-service Gem for a fictional company. I will supply my own decisions and the policy. Help me turn them into clear reusable instructions. Keep behaviour separate from company facts. Do not invent policies or permissions. Point out any unclear boundary. MY DECISIONS: [paste your notes]

The detailed setup route, including local-file alternatives, is in the Google guide. Menu wording and availability can change; the learning goal is the configured assistant, not a particular button label.

Theory connection: D1-D3: instructions guide behaviour; a source supplies facts. This is a configured assistant, not an autonomous system that changes orders.

Output: Your own saved Gem contains instructions and one approved policy source.

Test five questions and verify each one

Open Level 1 in the Evidence Lab

  1. Copy one question from this level of the reporting app and ask your saved Gem. Use a new conversation with the same Gem for each main test and for an important retest, so an earlier pasted answer does not supply missing knowledge.
  2. Read the response. Record Correct / Partly correct / Incorrect or no answer for the first attempt. Write a short summary of what it actually said. A refusal can be correct when the source says a person must decide.
  3. Open the original policy, email, call or register. Select every source you personally checked and enter an exact ID, section or field. A citation displayed by Gemini is not a substitute for reading that source.
  4. If the answer is wrong or incomplete, locate the missing information, improve the appropriate knowledge or instruction and retest. Keep the first result and record the latest result; do not rewrite the first attempt as a success.
  5. If a genuine tool limitation remains, record it honestly. Do not tick Correct merely to finish. A reporting app can check that fields are filled in; it cannot mark the content for the teacher.

Use the policy section IDs as evidence, not an AI-generated summary of the policy. In this level, a refusal to approve a refund or a statement that an estimate is not guaranteed may be the correct answer.

Output: Five first results, five checked original locations and any retests are recorded.

Add two screenshots and your short notes

  1. Screenshot 1: your saved Gem instructions, focusing on sources, missing information and human limits.
  2. Screenshot 2: the Knowledge area showing your policy file. Hide personal account details.

In the two closing boxes, briefly say what you checked or improved and what the assistant must not do. Do not paste a full chat. The app checks report space and will ask you to shorten text rather than silently remove it.

Create, check and submit your one-page PDF

Open the app's A4 preview. Keep answer summaries short but retain the important conditions. Upload two readable image crops, not an entire desktop. The PDF uses screenshots first, followed by the five test rows and the closing notes.

Use Download Level 1 PDF. Open the downloaded file and check your name, all five rows and both screenshots. The required file name is D1-Knowledge-Assistant.pdf.

Add this PDF to the same Theme D submission in Moodle. Keep the earlier level PDFs there; you may return before the deadline to add another level. If Moodle unexpectedly locks the submission, ask the teacher to reopen it rather than starting a new submission elsewhere.

Aim to complete one level in one sitting. If you must stop, download a backup that includes your images. Your Gem and source files live in your own Google account; the reporting app's draft is local and is not linked to Google Drive, OneDrive or Moodle.

Technical problems: the equivalent submission route

Use the Level 1 alternative Word template if the app or PDF export fails. Supply the same answers, original references, screenshots and short notes. A second page is allowed in the fallback when needed for legibility. The technical route does not reduce your mark.

First preserve your work with Download backup, or use Download report as HTML / Open print alternative. Do not clear your browser data as a first troubleshooting step. If internet disconnects after the app has loaded, keep its tab open; Gemini and the Moodle upload need a connection even though the report can be saved locally.
