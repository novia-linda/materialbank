E01 | 2026-06-01 | Aino Ranta | Collection after work
Customer: Hello, I finish work at 16:30 and cannot get to your Vaasa terminal in the morning. My notice says the parcel is ready. What time can I collect it? I might be able to leave work a little early on Friday.
Nordic Freight / Maja: Hello Aino. Collection at the Vaasa terminal is available Monday to Friday, 08:00-17:00. On Saturday the collection desk is open 09:00-12:00. It is closed on Sunday. These collection hours apply only after the parcel is Ready for collection and a collection notice has been sent. Please bring the shipment reference and photo identification.

E02 | 2026-06-02 | Joel Niemi | Crushed corner on delivery
Customer: Hello, one corner of the box is crushed and I think the item inside may also be damaged. I received it this morning. Should I wait until I have checked everything before I contact you?
Nordic Freight / Susan: Please contact Customer Service promptly, preferably by the next working day after you discover the damage. This is our internal reporting request, not a statement that your legal rights expire then. Give us the shipment reference and explain what was damaged. Keep the item and all packaging while Claims Team reviews the case. Our phone team can explain which photographs are useful. A report is not an automatic compensation decision.

E03 | 2026-06-02 | Emil West | Different delivery address
Customer: My plans have changed. Could my parcel go to my office instead of my home? The tracking page still says In transit. I can send the office address now.
Nordic Freight / Johan: We can submit an address-change request, but Transport Operations must approve it. Send the shipment reference, the original address, the requested new address and a contact telephone number. Do not assume the delivery date stays the same. If the status has already changed to Out for delivery, Customer Service must check with Transport Operations before promising any change; we cannot guarantee redirection that day.

E04 | 2026-06-03 | Sara Holm | Away for a few days
Customer: I have just received a collection notice, but I will be away until next week. How long do you hold parcels? Do Saturday and Sunday count as days?
Nordic Freight / Maja: We hold a collection parcel for five working days, counted from the working day after the collection notice is sent. For this rule, working days mean Monday to Friday, excluding Finnish public holidays. Saturday does not count even though the collection desk is open then, and Sunday does not count. Contact Customer Service before the holding period ends if you need more time. An extension needs approval; it is not automatic.

E05 | 2026-06-04 | Noora Salmi | Several boxes with our company name
Customer: Good afternoon. We sent two orders and both labels show our company name. Can you check by the name alone? I have a photograph of one of the labels but not the other.
Nordic Freight / Elias: A company name alone may match more than one shipment. Please send the ShipmentID from the label or booking confirmation for each shipment. Keep the references separate when you ask about two orders. The reference helps us avoid mixing up status, destination and recipient information. If you cannot find it, telephone Customer Service; they can collect the details needed to look for the correct record.

E07 | 2026-06-08 | Mira Aalto | Someone else collecting
Customer: My brother can pass the terminal tomorrow and save me a long trip. May he collect the parcel for me? He does not have my identity card and I do not want to give it to him.
Nordic Freight / Susan: Another person can collect an eligible parcel with your written authorisation, the shipment reference and their own photo identification. The collection desk checks the authorisation. Please arrange it before the person visits; we cannot replace this with an unverified statement at the desk.

E11 | 2026-06-11 | Anton Back | Do weekend days use up the holding period?
Customer: My collection message arrived today. The weekend is nearly here. Will those two days use up my time, and can I simply leave the parcel there for an extra week?
Nordic Freight / Elias: The five-working-day holding period starts on the working day after the collection notice. Monday to Friday count, except Finnish public holidays. Neither Saturday nor Sunday counts. The Saturday collection opening does not change that calculation. Please contact Customer Service before the period ends to request an extension; you need approval rather than assuming that an extra week is available.

E12 | 2026-06-11 | Nina Lehto | Written confirmation for our office
Customer: Our office needs something in writing after a delivery. Could you send proof that the shipment has been handed over? I do not need a new booking, only confirmation for our records.
Nordic Freight / Susan: Customer Service can arrange a written delivery confirmation after checking the shipment reference and the available delivery record. Ask for it using the ShipmentID. The assistant can explain this process but cannot send the confirmation itself. If the customer disputes the Delivered status, the record must first be investigated rather than being treated as proof that the customer personally received or located the parcel.
