# Theme C bonus case – design review after first build

## What is working
- Each candidate has a distinct, defensible performance story.
- Each core detective source contains evidence about all four candidates.
- The sources conflict in productive ways instead of producing one obvious ranking.
- The fifth source corroborates the driver perspective without containing a secret fact that four-person groups cannot access.
- The case can be solved with ordinary comparisons, filtering, averages, totals, percentages and qualitative theme analysis; no advanced statistics are required.

## Deliberate tensions
- Johan: revenue / new-business strength versus discounts, rework and downstream quality.
- Maja: lower volume and slow raw case time versus extreme case complexity, rescue work and trusted execution.
- Susan: exceptional customer experience versus throughput and time per case.
- Elias: operational consistency and process contribution versus lower visibility and fewer standout customer comments.

## Things to pilot in class
1. Twenty minutes of parallel detective work may be tight for students who are new to Copilot file analysis. If needed, extend the detective phase to 25 minutes and shorten the final formatting phase.
2. The sales file contains enough fields to tempt students to focus only on revenue. This is intentional, but the role questions must explicitly require contribution margin and cost analysis.
3. Maja's CRM profile is intentionally context-dependent. Check that students notice Complexity, EscalatedToEmployee and PreviousHandlersCount rather than simply calling her slow.
4. The optional fifth source should feel like corroboration, not a duplicate. About 20–30% of its comments concern team/process contributions that drivers cannot observe directly.
5. The final output should stay short. One page/slide per candidate plus one recommendation page/slide is enough.

## Recommendation before final Moodle build
Pilot the raw data and the role prompts once. If one candidate repeatedly becomes the obvious winner without discussion, rebalance the numerical data before creating screenshots or a final renderer.
