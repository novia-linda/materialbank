Cross-source findings students may discover

  • Johan's high sales are genuine, but his headline revenue overstates his total contribution when discounts, correction costs and downstream rework are considered.
  • Maja's lower case volume and longer resolution time are strongly affected by case mix: she receives far more advanced/critical, escalated and previously handled cases.
  • Susan's slower throughput is consistent with the qualitative evidence that she spends more time listening, explaining and following up; the same behaviour is a weakness in efficiency and a strength in customer experience.
  • Elias is rarely the most visible candidate but is strong across economics, routine operational efficiency, data quality and process contribution.
  • Agreement between driver feedback and other staff feedback provides corroboration, but repeated opinions are still not the same as objective proof.

Suggested oral questions

  1. Which number makes Johan look strongest at first, and which number makes that conclusion less simple?
  2. Why is NetRevenueEUR alone a weak basis for the bonus decision?
  3. Who had the highest customer satisfaction, and what trade-off appears elsewhere in the data?
  4. What do the customer comments add that the rating averages do not?
  5. Why could Maja look inefficient if you only use average resolution time?
  6. Which fields show that Maja receives a different case mix from the others?
  7. What evidence suggests that Johan creates downstream work for other people?
  8. Do the drivers and internal staff tell the same story? Give one example.
  9. What is the strongest argument for Elias even though he is not the top performer on every headline KPI?
  10. What is the strongest argument against giving the bonus to Elias?
  11. What is the strongest argument for Susan?
  12. What is the strongest argument against Susan if the company prioritises capacity and throughput?
  13. Which candidate benefits most when you adjust your interpretation for context?
  14. Which finding appears in more than one independent source?
  15. Where do the data sources disagree or emphasise different values?
  16. What is the strongest counterargument against your chosen winner?
  17. Who was your runner-up and what evidence kept that person from first place?
  18. What additional information would you want before making a real HR bonus decision?
  19. If the company valued new-customer growth above all else, would your decision change?
  20. If the company valued operational reliability above all else, would your decision change?
