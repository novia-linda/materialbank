============================================================
CORE MASTER PROMPT – DAY 1
============================================================

Build a responsive bilingual web app for the fictional training company **Vasa Business Academy**.

This is Day 1 of a multi-day course prototype. Today, build ONLY the customer-facing frontend. Do not build any backend, database, Google Sheets connection, authentication or payment integration yet.

## COMPANY

**Vasa Business Academy** is a small fictional training company that offers short, practical courses and workshops to companies, organisations and individuals.

Typical customers are small and medium-sized companies that want to develop practical skills among their staff.

Training can be organised online or at the customer’s premises.

Swedish company introduction:
“Vasa Business Academy erbjuder korta och praktiska utbildningar för företag och organisationer. Utbildningarna kan ordnas online eller på plats hos kunden. Målet är att göra nya kunskaper enkla att förstå och använda i arbetet.”

English company introduction:
“Vasa Business Academy offers short and practical training for companies and organisations. Training can be delivered online or at the customer’s premises. The goal is to make new knowledge easy to understand and apply at work.”

## LANGUAGE

The complete user interface must support **Swedish and English**.

Add a clear language switcher: **SV | EN**.

The switch must change all user-visible:
- navigation
- headings
- descriptions
- buttons
- form labels
- validation messages
- cart text
- checkout text
- confirmation text

Do not duplicate the entire application into two separate sites. Use one application with structured translations.

Product IDs such as P001 must remain unchanged in both languages.

## CART

Build a frontend-only shopping cart for the three evening courses.

The customer must be able to:
- add courses
- see Product ID
- see course name
- see unit price
- change quantity
- remove an item
- see total price update immediately

Important Day 1 rule:

**The cart must exist only in the current frontend/browser session.**

Do NOT use localStorage, database storage or another persistent storage method.

After a full page reload, the cart must be empty.

## CHECKOUT

Add a checkout form with:

- Name – required
- Email – required
- Company – optional
- Street address – required
- Postal code – required
- City – required
- Order note – optional

Add simple validation:
- required fields must not be empty
- email must look like a valid email address

Show clear validation messages in the currently selected language.

Button:
**Send order / Skicka beställning**

## CONFIRMATION

After a valid frontend-only checkout, show a confirmation view.

Show:
- a temporary fictional order number
- customer name
- selected course(s)
- quantity
- unit price
- total

Make it clear that this is only the current prototype session and the order has not yet been saved.

Swedish:
“Det här är ännu en frontend-prototyp. Beställningen har inte sparats permanent.”

English:
“This is still a frontend prototype. The order has not been saved permanently.”

Day 1 is complete when the bilingual customer-facing experience works from browsing courses through cart, checkout and frontend-only confirmation.
